Common questions

  • Why does the keyboard need "Full Access"? iOS only allows a custom keyboard to read the shared, on-device storage it uses to talk to the main AudioToText app — where your dictated text comes from — once Full Access is granted. It's not used for anything else. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • Does dictation work offline? Yes, once a model is downloaded. Apple's built-in Speech engine needs no download at all; the optional Parakeet models are downloaded once and then work fully offline.
  • Is my audio or text sent anywhere? No. All transcription and summarization runs on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
