Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

AudioToText is an on-device dictation, transcription, and note-taking app for iOS. All speech recognition and AI summarization run entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your audio, transcripts, or any personal data on any server — there is nothing to send, because the app has no server.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Microphone. Used only to capture audio for on-device transcription. Audio is processed locally and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Speech recognition. Transcription runs using either Apple's built-in, on-device Speech framework, or an optional NVIDIA Parakeet model running locally via Apple's CoreML — your choice. Both run entirely on-device; neither sends audio or text off your phone.
  • AI summarization. Note summaries are generated entirely on-device using Apple's Foundation Models framework. No transcript or summary is sent to any external service.
  • Keyboard "Full Access". iOS keyboard extensions cannot access the microphone directly, and are only allowed to read the App Group — a small piece of storage shared between the keyboard and the main app on your device — once Full Access is granted. AudioToText uses this solely so the keyboard can tell when a dictation session is active and read back the transcribed text; it is not used for network access, analytics, or any other purpose.
  • Network access for model downloads. If you choose to use an optional Parakeet model, the app downloads that model's weights (a file, not your data) from Hugging Face's servers. This is a one-way download — no audio, text, or personal information is ever uploaded as part of it. Apple's built-in Speech model requires no download.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party data sharing — there's no data collected to share.
  • No cloud storage of recordings, transcripts, or summaries — they stay in the app's local storage on your device, under your control (and removed if you delete them or delete the app).

Children's privacy

AudioToText is not directed at children under 13, and since the app does not collect data from any user, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
