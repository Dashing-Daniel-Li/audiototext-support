The agreement

AudioToText is licensed to you under Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. That agreement, at apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula, is the contract between you and the developer for your use of the app. This page adds the specifics of AudioToText's free tier and its paid plans; where the two say different things, Apple's agreement wins.

The developer is Daniel Li, who publishes AudioToText on the App Store under the name Sotto Voce. Purchases are made through Apple and are governed by Apple's terms as well.

The free tier

Without a subscription you can dictate up to 1,000 words a week through the keyboard. Words are counted when the keyboard types them into a field, and the allowance resets every week. Recording, transcribing and summarising audio inside the app are not limited by this allowance.

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase

Pro removes the weekly word limit. It is offered three ways; the price of each is shown in the app in your local currency before you buy, and is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.

  • Pro Monthly — an auto-renewable subscription, billed every month.
  • Pro Annual — an auto-renewable subscription, billed every year, with a free trial (see below).
  • Pro Lifetime — a one-off purchase that never renews and never expires.

A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends, at the price shown in the app at the time. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your device's Settings, under your Apple Account, in Subscriptions.

Monthly and Annual are the same subscription at two billing intervals: switching between them is done through Apple, and the change takes effect at the next renewal.

Free trial

Pro Annual starts with a 7-day free trial when one is offered to you. The trial converts to a paid annual subscription at the end of the seven days unless you cancel at least 24 hours before then. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription. Apple decides who is eligible for an introductory offer, and a trial is generally available once per Apple Account.

Cancelling and refunds

Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not end the period you have already paid for, which runs to its scheduled end with Pro still active. Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple under its own policy — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Restoring a purchase on another device is done from the app's Settings, with Restore Purchases.

Accuracy

Transcripts and summaries are made on your device and can contain mistakes. Check anything important before you rely on it. AudioToText is a writing tool; it is not a substitute for professional advice, and is provided as described in Apple's agreement above.

Privacy

Everything you say or type stays on your device — there are no accounts and no servers of ours. The Privacy Policy explains what the app stores and why the keyboard asks for Full Access.

Changes to these terms

If the plans or the allowance change, this page is updated and the date below moves. A change to what a subscription costs is always shown to you by Apple before it takes effect, and you can cancel before it does.
